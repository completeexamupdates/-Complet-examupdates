Educational Qualification

1. Trade Apprentice – Technical Trades

  • Passed Class 10th / High School from a recognized Board.
  • Passed 2-year ITI course in the relevant trade.
  • ITI qualification should be recognized by NCVT / SCVT, as applicable.

Relevant Trades:

  • Fitter
  • Electrician
  • Electronics Mechanic
  • Instrument Mechanic
  • Machinist

2. Trade Apprentice – Data Entry Operator

  • Passed Class 12th / Intermediate.
  • Fresher candidates must meet the prescribed marks requirement.
  • Skilled candidates should possess the required NSQF-recognized Domestic Data Entry Operator Skill Certificate, wherever applicable.

3. Technician Apprentice

Candidates should possess a 3-year regular full-time Diploma in the relevant Engineering discipline from a recognized Institute/University.

  • Mechanical Engineering
  • Electrical Engineering
  • Instrumentation Engineering
  • Civil Engineering
  • Electrical & Electronics Engineering
  • Electronics Engineering
Minimum Marks:
General / EWS / OBC-NCL – 50%
SC / ST / PwBD – 45% for reserved positions, wherever applicable.

4. Graduate Apprentice

Candidates should possess a recognized Bachelor's Degree such as:

  • BA
  • B.Com
  • B.Sc.
  • BBA
  • Other eligible graduate qualifications as specified by IOCL
Minimum Marks:
General / EWS / OBC-NCL – 50%
SC / ST / PwBD – 45% for reserved positions, wherever applicable.
