📖 SSC CHSL Tier-I Syllabus

English Language

Spot the Error, Fill in the Blanks, Synonyms/Homonyms, Antonyms, Spelling, Idioms & Phrases, One Word Substitution, Sentence Improvement, Active/Passive Voice, Direct/Indirect Speech, Cloze Passage और Comprehension।

General Intelligence

Analogy, Classification, Venn Diagram, Series, Coding-Decoding, Figural Classification, Problem Solving, Critical Thinking, Embedded Figures आदि।

Quantitative Aptitude

Number System, Percentage, Ratio & Proportion, Average, Interest, Profit & Loss, Discount, Partnership, Mixture & Alligation, Time & Distance, Time & Work, Algebra, Geometry, Mensuration, Trigonometry और Charts।

General Awareness

Current Affairs, History, Culture, Geography, Economic Scene, General Policy, Scientific Research तथा भारत एवं पड़ोसी देशों से संबंधित सामान्य जानकारी।
